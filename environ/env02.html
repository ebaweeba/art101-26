e.e. cummings once said:

    "damn everything but the circus!
    damn everything that is grim,
    dull,
    motionless,
    unrisking,
    inward turning;
    damn everything that won't get into the circle,
    that won't enjoy.
    that won't throw its heart into the.
    tension,
    surprise,
    fear
    and delight of the circus,
    the round world,
    the full existence.
